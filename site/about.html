About

Bringing the world's work home to South Africa.

South Africa has a deep pool of skilled, English-speaking professionals and too few jobs for them. Sling connects that talent with firms abroad.

The idea

Don't just send a donation. Send the work.

In the Maimonides tradition, the highest form of giving is a job. Sling is a commercial service: clients get skilled, managed teams, and every team is jobs created at home.
