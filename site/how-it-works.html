How it works

One pilot. Ninety days. Clear KPIs.

You start small, with three to five roles, and decide at day 90 whether to scale, refine or stop.

  1. 01

    Days 0–30

    Design

    • Select the process to move
    • Set scope, KPIs and security
    • Agree pricing and governance
  2. 02

    Days 31–60

    Build

    • We recruit and shortlist
    • You interview and select
    • We onboard people and systems
  3. 03

    Days 61–90

    Prove

    • Go live with weekly QA
    • Track quality, savings and uptime
    • Decide to scale, refine or stop

Who does what

We run the team. You run the work.

Sling provides

  • Recruitment and employment
  • Workspace and equipment
  • Backup power and internet
  • Quality assurance and retention

You provide

  • Process documentation
  • Access to your systems
  • A named manager for the team

Pricing

One monthly fee per team member.

No recruitment fees. Pricing depends on role and seniority, so every engagement starts from a pilot quote. Indicative savings are 40–55% below equivalent US/UK hiring.

Questions

What clients ask us.

What contract do we sign?

A services agreement with Sling for the pilot, plus a data processing agreement. Sling is the employer, so you do not take on any South African employment obligations.

What is the notice period?

After the pilot, the notice period is set in the services agreement. [Notice period to be confirmed.]

Who owns the work?

You do. All intellectual property created by your team for you belongs to you, and this is written into the agreement.

How is our data protected?

The team works inside your systems, on managed devices, with access you control. We comply with POPIA and UK GDPR and sign a data processing agreement with every client.

What about public holidays?

South African public holidays are planned for in advance, and cover is agreed with you during the Design phase.

What happens during load-shedding?

Our workspace has backup power and backup internet, so the team keeps working through power cuts.

What if a team member is not the right fit?

We replace them at no extra recruitment cost, and manage the handover.

Which hours will the team work?

Yours. South Africa is one to two hours ahead of the UK, so the working day is almost fully shared. US coverage is arranged by shift.
